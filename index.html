Professional Profile

A highly experienced Principal Technical Delivery Manager with over 19 years of NHS and digital health experience, including 14 years working across two acute trusts and more than five years delivering complex architecture programmes at a national level. Bringing deep expertise in Electronic Patient Record (EPR) implementations, clinical systems integration and cloud-based solution design, underpinned by a strong track record of senior stakeholder engagement and multi-vendor programme governance. Skilled at translating complex technical requirements into clear strategic direction, working across clinical, operational, and digital teams to ensure coherent, standards-aligned solutions are delivered at pace and aligned to programme objectives.
